`mode="single"` selects one day. Pass `defaultMonth` so the calendar opens on the month you expect rather than on today.

## Range

`mode="range"` selects a start and an end day. `numberOfMonths` shows several months side by side.

## Dropdowns and disabled days

`captionLayout="dropdown"` swaps the month title for month and year dropdowns, bounded by `startMonth` and `endMonth`. `disabled` takes any DayPicker matcher; here it rules out weekends.

## Accessibility

- The month is a grid: arrow keys move by day and week, Page Up and Page Down by month, Home and End to the start and end of the week.
- Only the focused day is in the tab order, so Tab moves between the navigation buttons and the grid rather than through every day.
- Selected days carry `aria-selected`, and disabled days cannot be focused or chosen.
- Pass a `locale` to get translated month and weekday names and the correct first day of the week.
