## Usage

<Example name="drawer/usage">

Drag the handle down, or flick it, to dismiss. The content is full width, so centre a narrower column inside it on large screens, as here.

## Direction

`direction` on `Drawer` opens it from another edge. Left and right drawers take three quarters of the width, up to `max-w-sm`, and have no handle. For a side panel that does not need dragging, [Sheet](/docs/components/sheet) is the simpler choice.

	<Direction />
</Example>

## Accessibility

- It is a modal dialog: focus moves in and is trapped, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the trigger.
- Dragging is never the only way out. Keep a close or cancel button, as both examples do, for keyboard and switch users.
- `DrawerTitle` names the drawer and `DrawerDescription` describes it. Keep the title, hiding it with `sr-only` if the design has none.
