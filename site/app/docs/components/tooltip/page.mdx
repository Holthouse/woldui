## Usage

<Example name="tooltip/usage">
	<Usage />
</Example>

Tooltips need a `TooltipProvider` above them. Put one near the root of your app, once. WoldUI's provider sets `delayDuration` to `0`, so tooltips open straight away.

## With a shortcut

A `Kbd` inside the content is styled to sit on the dark tooltip, which makes it a good place to teach a keyboard shortcut. `side` places the tooltip on another edge of the trigger.

<Example name="tooltip/with-kbd">
	<WithKbd />
</Example>

## Accessibility

- It opens when the trigger is hovered or receives keyboard focus, and closes on Escape, blur or pointer leave.
- The content is linked to the trigger with `aria-describedby`, so it is read as a description, not a name. An icon-only button still needs its own `aria-label`.
- Keep it to a few words, and never put anything interactive or essential in it: touch screens cannot hover.
