## Sizes and states

`size` is `default` or `sm`. `disabled` and `aria-invalid` work as they do on any native select.

<Example name="native-select/sizes-and-states">
	<SizesAndStates />
</Example>

<Callout tone="warning">
	`className` styles the wrapper around the `<select>`, not the `<select>` itself. Every other prop, including `id` and `aria-*`, goes to the `<select>`.
</Callout>

## Accessibility

- It is a native `<select>`, so keyboard, typeahead and screen reader support come from the browser.
- Label it with a `Label` whose `htmlFor` matches the select's `id`, or with `aria-label`.
- The chevron is decorative and hidden from assistive technology.
