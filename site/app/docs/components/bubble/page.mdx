`align="end"` pushes a bubble to the right, for the current user's messages. `BubbleGroup` stacks consecutive bubbles. Inside a [Message](/docs/components/message) with `align="end"`, bubbles follow it without their own `align`.

## Variants

`variant` sets the surface. `ghost` drops the surface and padding altogether, for long replies that read better as plain text.

<Example name="bubble/variants">

</Example>

## Reactions

`BubbleReactions` pins a small pill to the bubble's edge. `side` puts it on the `top` or `bottom` edge and `align` on the `start` or `end` corner. It hangs outside the bubble, so leave room with a larger gap.

<Example name="bubble/reactions">

</Example>

## Accessibility

- Bubbles are plain `<div>`s. Put a sequence of messages in a list or a `role="log"` container, so a screen reader knows where the conversation starts and ends.
- Alignment is visual only. Say who sent a message in text, for example in a `MessageHeader`.
- `BubbleContent asChild` can render a `<button>` or `<a>`, and it then gets a hover state and a focus ring.
- Emoji reactions read poorly. Hide the emoji with `aria-hidden` and describe the reaction in `sr-only` text.
