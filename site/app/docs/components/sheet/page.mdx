`SheetHeader` and `SheetFooter` carry their own padding, so pad any content you put between them to match. The footer sits at the bottom of the panel. Like [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog), the content has a close button in the corner, which `showCloseButton={false}` removes.

## Sides

`side` sets the edge it slides in from: `right` (the default), `left`, `top` or `bottom`. Left and right sheets take the full height and three quarters of the width, up to `max-w-sm`. Top and bottom sheets take the full width and the height of their content.

## Accessibility

- It is a Radix Dialog underneath: focus moves in and is trapped, Escape or a click on the backdrop closes it, and focus returns to the trigger.
- `SheetTitle` is required as the sheet's accessible name. Hide it with `className="sr-only"` if the design has no visible title.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it appears in place without sliding.
