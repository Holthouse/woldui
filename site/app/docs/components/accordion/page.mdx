## Usage

<Example name="accordion/usage">
	<Usage />
</Example>

`type="single"` keeps one section open at a time. `collapsible` lets the open one close again; without it, one section always stays open.

## Multiple

`type="multiple"` lets any number of sections stay open. `defaultValue` takes an array of item values, and `disabled` on an item keeps it shut.

<Example name="accordion/multiple">
	<Multiple />
</Example>

## Accessibility

- Each trigger is a `<button>` inside a heading, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` pointing at its panel.
- Enter and Space toggle a section. Arrow Up and Down move between triggers, Home and End jump to the first and last.
- The height animation on open and close is skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
