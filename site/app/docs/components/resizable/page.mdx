`withHandle` draws a visible grip on the divider. `minSize` and `maxSize` keep a panel within bounds.

<Callout tone="warning">
	In react-resizable-panels v4, a number for `defaultSize`, `minSize` or `maxSize` means **pixels**.
	Pass a string (`"35"` or `"35%"`) for a percentage.
</Callout>

## Vertical and nested

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the panels. A group nests inside a panel to split it the other way.

## Accessibility

- Each handle is a focusable `separator` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`, so its position is announced.
- With a handle focused, the arrow keys resize the panels, and Home and End move it to either limit.
- The handle has no visible label. Give it an `aria-label` that names what it resizes.
- The focus ring on the handle is a 1px ring; keep a contrasting border around the group so it stays visible.
