## Usage

<Example name="popover/usage">
	<Usage />
</Example>

`PopoverContent` takes Radix's positioning props, such as `side`, `align` and `sideOffset`, and flips to stay on screen. `PopoverAnchor` positions it against a different element from the trigger.

## Accessibility

- It opens on click, Enter or Space. Focus moves into the content, and Escape or a click outside closes it and returns focus to the trigger.
- It is not modal: focus is not trapped, and Tab can move past the popover back into the page.
- `PopoverTitle` is a styled `<div>`, not a heading, and is not wired to the popover as its name. Give `PopoverContent` an `aria-label` when the content needs one.
