Collapsible is unstyled: it only toggles. `CollapsibleTrigger asChild` puts the behaviour on your own button, and anything outside `CollapsibleContent` stays visible.

## Controlled

Pass `open` and `onOpenChange` to own the state, for example to change the trigger's label. Use `defaultOpen` to start open without controlling it.

<Example name="collapsible/controlled">
	<Controlled />
</Example>

## Accessibility

- The trigger gets `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, pointing at the content.
- Enter and Space toggle it, like any button.
- An icon-only trigger needs an `aria-label` that says what it reveals.
- Closed content is removed from the page, so it is skipped by the tab order and by find-in-page.
