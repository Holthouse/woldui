## Accessibility

- It renders a `<button role="switch">` with `aria-checked`. Space and Enter toggle it, and it joins the tab order.
- Give it a `Label` with a matching `htmlFor`. Clicking the label toggles the switch, and the hit area extends past the track.
- Keep the label constant ("Auto-save drafts"), not "On" or "Off": the state is already announced.
- Link help text with `aria-describedby` so it is read when the switch takes focus.
- The focus ring is a 2px outline offset from the track, on keyboard focus only. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the thumb moves without its spring.
