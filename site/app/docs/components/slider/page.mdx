## Range

Two values make two thumbs. `minStepsBetweenThumbs` keeps them apart. Show the current value in text next to the slider, since the thumbs carry no label of their own.

## Accessibility

- Each thumb is a focusable `role="slider"` with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuenow`.
- The arrow keys move a thumb by one `step`, Page Up and Page Down by ten steps, and Home and End to the ends.
- With two thumbs, Radix names them "Minimum" and "Maximum".
- For values that need units ("9:00", "40%"), pass `getAriaValueText` or show the value in visible text.

<Callout tone="warning">
	A single-thumb slider's thumb has no accessible name. `aria-label` and `aria-labelledby` land on
	the root, not on the thumb, and `Slider` does not expose the thumb. Until it does, keep a visible
	label and value next to the slider.
</Callout>
