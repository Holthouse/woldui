`align="end"` mirrors the row for the current user's messages, and a [Bubble](/docs/components/bubble) inside follows it to the right. `MessageAvatar` sits at the bottom of the row, beside the last line.

## With a footer

`MessageFooter` holds a timestamp or a read receipt under the content. The avatar lifts to stay level with the bubble rather than the footer.

## Accessibility

- Messages are plain `<div>`s. Give the conversation `role="log"` and an `aria-label`, so new messages are announced politely as they arrive.
- Say who sent each message in text, with `MessageHeader` or in the footer. Alignment and avatars are visual only.
- The header and footer text is small (10px). Keep it to short metadata, not content anyone must read.
