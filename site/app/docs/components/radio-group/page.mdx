## Horizontal

`orientation="horizontal"` makes Left and Right the navigation keys; lay the items out in a row with `className`. A disabled item is skipped.

## Accessibility

- The group is one tab stop. Tab moves to the checked item, and the arrow keys move and select within the group.
- It renders `role="radiogroup"`. Name it with `aria-label`, or wrap it in a `FieldSet` with a `FieldLegend`.
- Each item needs a `Label` with a matching `htmlFor`; clicking the label selects the item.
- Inside a `<form>`, a hidden native radio carries the group's `name` and the chosen value.
