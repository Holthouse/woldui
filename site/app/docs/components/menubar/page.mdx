## Usage

<Example name="menubar/usage">
	<Usage />
</Example>

Each `MenubarMenu` is one trigger and its menu. The items are the same as [Dropdown Menu](/docs/components/dropdown-menu)'s: a `destructive` variant, checkbox and radio items, labels, shortcuts and submenus.

## Accessibility

- The bar is a single tab stop. Left and Right move between its triggers, and Enter, Space or Down opens a menu.
- While one menu is open, Left and Right open the neighbouring menus, as in a desktop app.
- Escape closes the open menu and returns focus to its trigger.
- `MenubarShortcut` only displays a shortcut. Bind the keys yourself if you show them.
